### Reading `Rack_Height` correctly

`Rack_Height` computes to **1950 mm** while the documented envelope height is
**1960 mm**. This is not a conflict. The model's bounding box runs **Z −10.0 to
+1950.0**: `Rack_Height` is the floor-datum-to-top dimension, and the feet extend
10 mm below Z = 0. Overall envelope height = 1950 + 10 = **1960 mm**.

Recorded here because it looks like a discrepancy every time someone checks it.

## 10. Change control

Any change to §2 (frame and envelope) or §1 (`Flood_Depth`) is a change to a
published contract in `trophic-contracts` and requires a decision record plus a
CHANGELOG entry there. The rest are internal.
