## 1. Criterion read of r2a (new condition)

C1 proportion marginal — the hose loop was the tallest, fattest element; the set read as "hose with fittings". C2 pass, no mimicry veto. **C3 visual weight in water: pass, the best criterion** — two thin verticals and one elbow; aged, three slot bands would read as three dark biofilm stripes. C4 pass (satin through-material). C5 marginal — five section changes within 40 mm on the dry side (socket step, stop bead, collar tab, guide foot, curl). **C6 mounting elegance: fail** — clip plus separate guide was a three-plate stack with visible black pad blocks; not obvious how it works, not worth photographing. **C7 rim crossing: fail** — pipe, socket, stub and hose did not read as one line; the guide curl was the most ornament-like element in the set. C8 marginal — tops at 20/10 and loops at 70/87 read mismatched, not intentional. C9 pass. C10 marginal — real hose splays; the design depended on the hose behaving. C11 pass — plain cap and dry swivel are good, keep both. C12 fail at the rim, pass below water.

## 2. Ranked simplifications and what r2b did with them

## 3. Where recognition lives (CMF recommendation → D-29)

The **clip as one folded stainless line at the rim**. Not the socket (plumbing-union language, close to ADA's swivel), not the termination. Everything else quiet: one socket step, one slot band, unmarked nozzle, plain cap.

## 4. Anti-mimicry

Closest approach is ADA Metal Pipe (swivel nozzle + removable plug); r2b stays clear because the swivel is dry, above the rim and formed from the tube, and the cap is flush. Removing the stop bead also cleared the Neo Flow "stacked joint" hint. No veto.

## 5. Engineering conflicts handed off

Manufacturing: one 60 mm slot band (web strength, laser distortion); formed-socket expansion sample. Systems: nozzle taper vs exit velocity (V-T9); swivel retention without the stop bead; hose kink radius and loop height (V-M1) and lid/light clearance (V-M2) — the hose stands ~74 mm above the rim, the pipes 25 mm. QA: no crevice below the waterline is unchanged by any of this; pads and any future joint remain brushable.

## 6. Open after this review

D-28 (hose loop: guided or specified), D-29 (cue = clip, to be confirmed on samples), taper-vs-reach (35), whether a recess for the pads is worth a forming operation, and the physical appearance review V-A1 on real parts under aquascape lights — a render cannot judge C10 or C12.
