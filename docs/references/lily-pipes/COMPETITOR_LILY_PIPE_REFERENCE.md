## 1. Summary

1. **The Indian market is served by imports at three price tiers and nobody designs for India.** Generic glass pairs sit at ₹1,599–2,199, generic stainless pairs with a skimmer at ₹2,790–3,999, Korean PETG Neo Flow at ₹2,499–4,150, Chihiros stainless at ₹3,000–4,600 per set (out of stock everywhere checked), a China-made "branded" stainless set at ₹5,000–6,000, and ADA glass at ₹14,500 for one piece. No Indian manufacturer of lily pipes was found. [R]
2. **Spec disclosure is poor and trust is low.** Most Indian listings publish no pipe length, tank depth range or glass thickness limit; warranty terms are almost never stated; the same Mufan stainless set is sold as grade 316 by two retailers and grade 304 by a third. [R] An honest, measured specification and a traceable material grade are differentiators by themselves.
3. **The recurring, costly problems are handling problems:** glass breakage during cleaning and hose removal, hose removal difficulty, the cleaning chore, skimmer air/noise/level sensitivity, and shrimp being drawn into intakes. Each has at least four independent sources. [F][R][B]
4. **Evidence of willingness to pay exists where people already spend to escape a problem:** rebuying glass then switching to stainless; buying mesh guards; replacing skimmer inflows with separate skimmers. Glass-vs-steel appearance, flow-pattern style and ADA prestige are preferences that segment buyers rather than problems that create willingness to pay.
5. **Market size is unknown.** No credible figure exists for Indian aquascaping hardware. §7 uses transparent bottom-up scenarios for Trophic's obtainable volume and lists the retailer data that would validate them.

**Interpretation [I] — PROPOSED, not decided.** The evidence points to a gap for hardware that survives handling, releases the hose without force, is easy to clean and stays good-looking over months, with a shrimp-conscious intake — and, separately, a skimmer that tolerates level change. Appearance preference (glass invisibility vs considered presence) splits segments and must be settled by interviews, not assumed.

### 5.2 Overseas prices vs landed cost — kept separate

- **Overseas retail** (Aquasabi EU, Buce Plant / Glass Aqua / GLA US) is a benchmark of what premium markets bear, not an Indian price. FX-only INR equivalents above ignore freight, duty and retail margin.
- **Estimated landed cost** applies only to buying finished OEM goods from China: e.g. a US$14 stainless set → ≈₹1,330 FOB + freight/clearance + ≈16.5% non-creditable duty → roughly ₹1,900–2,300 per set before Trophic packaging (ESTIMATE; see [COMMERCIAL_FEASIBILITY.md](../../../products/aquarium/lily-pipes/costing/COMMERCIAL_FEASIBILITY.md) §3 route M1 and [SOURCING_STRATEGY.md](../../../products/aquarium/lily-pipes/sourcing/SOURCING_STRATEGY.md) §3). Marketplace listings are not quotations.

### 5.3 Price-comparison pitfalls found

Per-piece vs pair (ADA, Chihiros Pro); skimmer and holders bundled or not (Neo Premium); tax and shipping included or not (Aqua Zones includes both, aquascaping.in excludes shipping, Aqua Club and Aquariums India state neither); fictitious or inverted "regular" prices (Chennai Aqua Studio shows sale above regular); category pages disagreeing with product pages (Aqua Club Chihiros Pro). Always normalise to a complete set including mounting before comparing.

## 6. What the market shows (context, not requirements)

1. **Nobody publishes compatibility honestly except ADA.** Flow per model and tank length per model is ADA-only; most sets omit length, immersion depth and glass thickness. [M][R]
2. **Stainless has won on durability but not on design.** It copies glass lily silhouettes and pairs them with clear acrylic holders (detailed in [LILY_PIPE_AESTHETIC_REFERENCE.md](LILY_PIPE_AESTHETIC_REFERENCE.md) §3).
3. **Spares and serviceability are visible only at Neo and Chihiros**; warranty is stated by one budget brand and explicitly denied by one premium-priced import.
4. **Premium-priced availability is unreliable.** Every Chihiros stainless SKU checked in India was out of stock on 2026-09-17. [R] This is one day's snapshot; do not read it as a trend.
5. **Integrated functions are appearing** (FZone CO₂ diffuser outlet at ₹3,000; Neo Reliever). Adjacent accessories exist but are not evidence of demand on their own.

## 7. Market attractiveness — without inventing a market size

**Known (FACT):** ADA India runs TGIAC, an IAPLC-authorised Indian contest with submissions 30 Sep–30 Oct 2026 and a ₹5,55,000 voucher prize pool ([tgiac.adaindia.in](https://tgiac.adaindia.in/), past entry counts show placeholder zeros). IAPLC 2025 had 1,533 entries from 77 countries/areas per an aggregator ([aqualibs](https://aqualibs.com/iaplc/2025/)); a handful of Indian-sounding names appear in its top 300, nationality unverified — low confidence. At least 13 Indian online stores carry lily pipes, typically with single- or double-digit stock per SKU. [R]

**Unknown:** number of planted-tank hobbyists, canister-filter installed base, lily-pipe unit sales, import volumes. Ornamental-fish market reports cover fish, not hardware, and are not used.

**Bottom-up scenarios for Trophic's obtainable first-year volume (HYPOTHESIS, not market size):**

| Scenario | DTC (own site / marketplace) | Specialist dealers | Year-1 sets | What would make it true |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 4 / month | 3 dealers × 1 / month | ≈ 84 | Weak brand awareness; dealers stock as a curiosity |
| Base | 10 / month | 8 dealers × 1.5 / month | ≈ 264 | Community seeding works; 8 of ~13+ observed online stores or equivalent local stores list it |
| Optimistic | 20 / month | 15 dealers × 2 / month | ≈ 600 | Product becomes a recommended upgrade in Indian forums/contests |

Using the base-case contribution per set (≈₹1,420 DTC, ≈₹620 dealer) and base fixed costs of ≈₹4.8 lakh ([COMMERCIAL_FEASIBILITY.md](../../../products/aquarium/lily-pipes/costing/COMMERCIAL_FEASIBILITY.md) §5, §7), year-1 contribution is roughly ₹0.9 lakh (conservative), ₹2.6 lakh (base) and ₹5.6 lakh (optimistic). Fixed costs would be recovered in about 10 months (optimistic), about 22 months (base), and not within five years (conservative). **The category is attractive as a low-capital, brand-building product line, not as a large profit engine** (PROPOSED interpretation).
