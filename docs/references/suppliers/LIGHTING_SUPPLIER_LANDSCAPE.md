## 0. Headline findings

1. **No aluminium extrusion press could be verified in Coimbatore.** Every Coimbatore "extrusion" entity found is a trader, retailer or fabricator of profiles pressed elsewhere. The nearest verified presses are Kerala (Hindalco Alupuram, Malabar), Chennai (KMC), Mysuru (Bhoruka), Bengaluru (Jindal). This is the most important sourcing fact for the shared aquarium body and the `LT-A` bar.
2. **Coimbatore is credible today for**: CNC end-machining, sheet-metal brackets and powder coating, injection-moulded end caps and their tooling, Type II anodising at pilot batch size (specs unverified), EPDM die-cut gaskets, non-moulded cable harnesses, corrugated/EPE/pulp packaging, stock stainless fasteners, pilot SMT/PCBA including MCPCB population (two candidates, qualification required), assembly and box-build.
3. **Elsewhere in India for**: extrusion press and die; MCPCB fabrication (Chennai, Hosur, Gandhinagar); PMMA/PC diffuser profile extrusion (Mumbai, Gujarat); BIS-registered LED drivers via Mean Well's official Indian distributors; BIS-registered adapters (NCR OEMs, Salcomp at scale); NABL photometry, photobiological and safety labs; scale EMS as fallback.
4. **Imported for Gen-1**: every LED emitter (binned horticultural white, 660 nm, high-CRI white, RGB), driver ICs, MCU and radio modules, precision lenses, IP-rated branded connectors.
5. **Supply events that change the option space (verified 2026-09-11):** Samsung exited the LED business (chip production ended H1 2026; LM301-family is sell-through only, do not design in); the San'an/Inari acquisition of Lumileds was abandoned on 2026-04-17 after CFIUS objection and Lumileds' San Jose fab closed (usable, never single-source); ams OSRAM sold its Entertainment and Industry Lamps unit to Ushio (closed 2026-03-02) but Oslon opto lines continue; Cree LED components belong to Penguin Solutions since 2021 and Cree Lighting's luminaire assets were auctioned in May 2026 (separate entity; verify component continuity).

### 3.2 Counterfeit and traceability

LED counterfeiting is documented (Osram Trust Program; fake "Samsung" diodes: https://primalgrowgear.com/2021/08/how-to-tell-real-samsung-led-diodes-from-fake/). Mitigation: buy only from manufacturer-listed authorised channels (AS6496 authorised distribution; AS6081 governs independents; AS6171 test methods, https://blog.ansi.org/anab/what-is-as6081/); require certificate of conformance and date/lot/bin codes on every reel label; incoming QC by spectroradiometer on a sample per reel (peak λ, CCT, Vf), a lab capability Trophic needs anyway.

## 4. Cross-cutting manufacturing notes

**End caps (all inference unless marked):** CNC-machined 6061/6063 has no tooling, no MOQ, anodises to a close match if processed in the same bath and batch, and machinable seal grooves; the lowest-risk path at 220 units. Pressure die-cast (ADC12/A380) makes sense at thousands and **anodises poorly** (high-silicon alloys go dark and inconsistent; usually powder-coated instead) [general metallurgy]. Injection-moulded PC/ABS/PA: simple moulds ₹2–8 lakh, 4–6 weeks [Moldrite, April 2026, https://www.moldrite.in/blog/injection-molding-cost-india.html], tool amortisation dominates at 440 pieces, gives a deliberate colour and texture contrast rather than a match, moulded gasket lands and TPE overmould possible. Sheet: very low cost, weak sealing.

**Modular vs potted electronics:** connectorised PSU, controller, LED and driver boards add roughly ₹20–60 per unit (wire-to-board pairs ₹3–10, 2–4 harnesses ₹15–40 each from a local harness shop, RFQ required) and assembly steps, but make repair a board swap and failure analysis possible. Potted or sealed electronics remove connector cost and improve condensation immunity but every field failure is a whole-unit replacement with no root cause. The common middle path: external SELV adapter (its warranty and compliance are the adapter maker's), connectorised LED and control boards, conformal coating on the control board only.
