# EDR-012 — Ooty room carries 11 racks with the water plant external

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | ACCEPTED |
| **Date** | 2026-09 |
| **Affects** | RK-A-ROOM |

## Context

The 20 x 12 ft room can be laid out at several densities depending on where the water plant sits and how much aisle is retained for service access. The layout had to be an engineering set-out with real clearances, not racks arranged visually.

## Decision

Adopt the **11-rack layout with the water plant on the terrace** (ADR-002), set out to coordinates.

| Option | Grow area | Floor multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| 9 racks, plant inside the room | - | 1.06x |
| **11 racks, plant on the terrace** | **29.0 m2** | **1.30x** |
| 12 racks, plant fully external | - | 1.42x |

## Consequences

- Room total cost Rs 1,321,506 (about Rs 13.2 lakh) excluding LED fixtures.
- Water balance at 11 racks / 44 beds: 1,914 L/day circulated, 235 L/day make-up, 87.7 % recovery.
- The 12-rack option was not taken: it requires moving distribution out of the room as well, and leaves insufficient service aisle.
- Set-out assumes the plenum-ready 690 mm installed depth even for Phase-1 racks (ND-04 confirms this).
