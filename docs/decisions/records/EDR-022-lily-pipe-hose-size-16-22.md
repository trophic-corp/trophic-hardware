# EDR-022 — `AQ-LP-A` launch hose size: 16/22 mm

## Context

Hose labels are inconsistent between suppliers; 12/16 and 16/22 (internal/external diameter, mm) cover most branded canisters. In Indian retail the in-stock value canister, Sunsun HW-302, is listed with 16/22 hose, and Sunsun models were in stock where most Eheim models were not. Oase BioMaster, Eheim Professionel 4+, Eheim Classic 600 and JBL e1502 also use 16/22. A 16 mm hose bore suits the 60–90 cm tank class at up to ≈1,000 l/h installed flow.

## Decision

The launch `AQ-LP-A` set is for **16 mm internal / 22 mm external diameter hose**. 12/16 is a later variant, not part of the launch.

## Consequences

- Pipe outside diameter, bore and hose-retention geometry are sized to 16/22 hose (tube size itself remains a target to confirm by RFQ and pull-off test).
- Compatibility claims require measured hoses (V-M1) and pull-off tests (V-T2); published labels are not sufficient.
- **X3 is not closed** for the whole aquarium range; `AQ-FL-A` should treat 16/22 as the default unless its own evidence says otherwise.
- If the filter survey (V-C2) shows 12/16 dominates the target market, this record is superseded, not edited.
