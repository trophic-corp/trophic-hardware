# ADR-002 — Locate the water plant on the terrace, outside the grow room

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | ACCEPTED |
| **Date** | 2026-09 |
| **Affects** | RK-A-WRS, RK-A-ROOM |

## Context

The initial layout placed source and recovery storage inside the 20 x 12 ft room. Tanks inside the room consume grow floor, add a large thermal mass and a humidity source into a controlled envelope, and put a standing water volume above electrical distribution.

## Decision

Move the entire water plant — source reservoir, recovery reservoir and the recovery skid — to the terrace. The room retains only the distribution main, the rack drops, the common drain and the sump trough.

The sump is a **surface trough in a corner** of the room, not a below-slab pit. The recovery pump sits at that low point and pushes up to the terrace (EDR-005).

## Consequences

- Frees room floor: the room goes from 9 racks (1.06x floor multiplier) with the plant inside, to **11 racks** with the plant external (1.30x, 29.0 m2 grow area). A 12-rack variant at 1.42x exists if the plant is fully external including distribution.
- Terrace slab at +3750 mm plus a 500 mm plinth gives an outlet at +4250 mm, which is what makes gravity feed viable (EDR-005).
- Adds an exposed outdoor run: UV-stable pipework and freeze/heat considerations for Ooty become a specification item.
- Requires terrace structural capacity for the filled tanks to be confirmed against the building record.
