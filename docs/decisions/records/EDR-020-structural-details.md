# EDR-020 — Structural details: foot insert, upright drainage, LED saddles, deck mesh

**Status:** ACCEPTED · **Type:** Engineering · **Affects:** `RK-A-DWG` CH01/05/10, `RK-A-MFG` BOM

1. **Foot insert `RK-A-108`:** zinc-plated steel M12 square tube insert for the 36.8 mm bore, press-fit in the upright bottom, replacing the bottom plastic cap. Foot `RK-A-105` unchanged.
2. **Upright drainage:** 2 × Ø6 holes at Z 55 on the two grid faces, below the base beam, so wash-down water inside the sealed tube drains. Top cap retained.
3. **LED saddles `RK-A-302`:** 2 mm GI end plate on the upright inner X face from the post grid line to the rail, 40 × 200 / 40 × 198 (front/rear variants), bolted to a rivet nut at grid rows 600/1000/1400/1800 (one row below each rail), with a 20 mm tab under the rail end. Rail `RK-A-301` shortened to **1172 mm**; fixture envelope 62–1194. Nylon isolating washer retained. Top tier uses the same saddle — no dedicated rail part.
4. **Deck mesh:** *flattened* expanded metal, 1.6 mm strand, ≥ 70 % open (LWD 30 × SWD 12 or equivalent), LWD along the 1176 span. The panel sits **on top of** the deck frame (Z 298.4–300.0) as modelled — EDR-001 datum stack confirmed; the "flush ≤ 1 mm" note in DWG CH10 is withdrawn. 20 mm folded edge on the two long sides only, folding downward outside the long rails.
