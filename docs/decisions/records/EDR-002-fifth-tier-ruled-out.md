# EDR-002 — Reject a fifth tier at 1900 mm on reach grounds

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | ACCEPTED |
| **Date** | 2026-09 |
| **Affects** | RK-A |

## Context

Adding a fifth tier is the cheapest way to increase grow area per square metre of floor. With a 400 mm pitch the fifth bed datum would sit at 1900 mm.

## Decision

Reject it. Four tiers with a 1500 mm top bed is the **reach-limited maximum**.

By the NIOSH revised lifting equation the recommended weight limit falls to zero above 1750 mm. A bed at 1900 mm cannot be loaded, harvested or cleaned within the equation's limits by an operator standing at the rack.

## Consequences

- Rack height is set at 1960 mm, which also keeps the rack under typical ceiling and door constraints.
- Grow-area density must be gained from floor layout (ADR-002, EDR-012) rather than from height.
- Any future taller variant requires a mechanical handling aid, not a re-argument of the ergonomics.

## Evidence

NIOSH revised lifting equation; `RK-A-QC_Rev3`
