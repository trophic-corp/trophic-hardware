# EDR-018 — Rear X-brace RK-A-104 re-derived on the grid

**Status:** ACCEPTED · **Type:** Engineering · **Supersedes:** EDR-010 · **Affects:** `RK-A-DWG` CH04, `RK-A-MFG` §07/§08

## Context

EDR-010's 1797 mm was the diagonal of the 1195 × 1342 bounding box, not the bar. The v8 bar was 1771.6 mm, had no holes, and its ends stopped at the upright inner faces; 1757 mm hole centres land on no grid pair (RK-A-REV F-04).

## Decision

Two separate bars, GI flat 25 × 3. Holes on the rear-upright centrelines (X 20 and X 1236) at grid rows **Z 150 and Z 1450**. Hole centres **1780.1 mm**, end distance 15 mm, **bar length 1810 mm**, angle 46.9°. Bar A at Y 563–566 lies on the rear gusset plates (EDR-015); bar B at Y 566–569 on 3 mm packers 40 × 25 at its ends. The bars cross without being joined. Rear panel `RK-A-501` moves to Y 569–571; plenum reserve starts at Y 571 (ICR-004 remains OPEN). Each end: one M8 × 25 into the plate's rivet nut, 18 N·m. Net section 48 mm² → 10 kN against 0.44 kN demand at 300 N.
