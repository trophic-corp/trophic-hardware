# EDR-023 — `AQ-LP-A` mounting range: rimless glass 5–12 mm; rimmed tanks excluded at launch

## Context

Reference rimless tanks span 4–15 mm glass; the 60–90 cm class targeted at launch is typically 5–10 mm, and market clamps commonly accept up to 12 mm. Rimmed-tank mounting is a recurring complaint that no single clip solves well. Indian local tank-builder glass practice is not yet surveyed.

## Decision

The launch set mounts on **rimless glass from 5 mm to 12 mm** thick. **Rimmed tanks are not supported at launch** and product information says so.

## Consequences

- Clip and hook geometry must cover 5–12 mm (the proposed method is two replaceable pad ranges; the method itself is a design choice, not part of this decision).
- Mount hold and glass-edge safety are verified at both 5 mm and 12 mm (V-T8).
- If the tank-builder survey (V-M3) shows common Indian tanks outside this range, a new record supersedes this one.
